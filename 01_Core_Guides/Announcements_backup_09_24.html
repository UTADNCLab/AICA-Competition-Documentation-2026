Questions and Answers

Q: In the QDrone2 tools, are linear_vel and angular_vel tunable parameters in the waypoint profiler?

A: Teams may modify any part of the waypoint profiler, including the linear_vel and angular_vel parameters. Teams are also welcome to develop and use their own waypoint profiler.

Q: Does the QCar2 need to follow a specific lane-side rule, such as always driving on the right side of the road?

A: The QCar2 should remain on the right side of the road.
